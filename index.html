Wicki Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to run Wicki, save your progress and fix bugs. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

Wicki is an educational app that teaches trading through lessons, quizzes and a simulator with pretend money. The data controller is Bahradine Adam, Åtvidaberg, Sweden. Contact: adambaradine4@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and, if you add one, your name or display nameTo create and sign in to your account and to send account emails (e.g. verification, password reset)
Account IDTo link your progress, settings and purchases to your account
Age or year of birthTo restrict high-risk lessons (such as CFDs and crypto) and simulator leverage to users aged 18+
Learning progress (lessons, XP, streaks, quiz answers, simulator activity)To save your progress and personalise your learning plan
App usage events (e.g. screens viewed, buttons tapped) and an app-generated device identifierTo understand how Wicki is used and improve it (analytics)
Approximate location (country/city derived from IP address)Aggregated analytics, e.g. which countries use the app
Crash reports and performance data (device model, OS version, error logs)To find and fix bugs
Subscription status for Wicki PlusTo unlock paid content. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, real financial information or microphone recordings. The money in the simulator is not real.

3. Services we use

We share data only with service providers that process it on our behalf:

  • Supabase — account login and database (stores your account and progress)
  • PostHog — product analytics
  • Sentry — crash reporting and performance monitoring
  • Resend — sending account emails
  • ElevenLabs — generating the voice of the Wicki mascot (no personal data is sent)
  • Apple — App Store distribution and in-app purchases

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU/EEA. When that happens, the transfer is protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Contract — to provide your account, progress and Wicki Plus
  • Legitimate interest — analytics and crash reporting to keep the app working and improve it
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to keep certain records

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted within 30 days. Analytics and crash data are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To delete your account, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account in the app, or email us. For any other request, contact adambaradine4@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

7. Children

Wicki is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Users aged 13–17 can use Wicki, but high-risk content is restricted.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with providers that use industry-standard security. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.

Sammanfattning på svenska

Wicki samlar bara in det som behövs för att appen ska fungera: e-post och konto, din ålder (för 18+-spärren), din progress, anonym användningsstatistik, ungefärlig plats via IP samt kraschrapporter. Vi säljer aldrig data, visar inga annonser och spårar dig inte. Du kan radera ditt konto under Profil → Inställningar → Radera konto eller genom att mejla adambaradine4@gmail.com.
