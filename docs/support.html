FAQ

Is Wicki free?

Yes. All lessons, practice, the simulator and the league are free.

Is this financial advice?

No. Wicki is education only. Simulator prices are made up, and the real charts are for practice. Nothing in the app tells you what to buy or sell.

Where does the market data come from?

Crypto prices: Powered by CoinGecko. Currency rates: European Central Bank (Source: ECB statistics). Prices can be delayed.

Do I need an account?

No. You can learn without one. A free account (email, Apple or Google) saves your progress across devices and lets you join the weekly league and ask Wicki AI.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, scroll down and tap “Delete my account”. Your account and synced data are removed right away. If you signed in with Apple, the Apple sign-in link is revoked too.

How do I turn off reminders?

Open Profile → Reminders, or turn off notifications for Wicki in your phone's settings.
